Sample templates use invented ports, prices, and vessels.

Rates — required: Origin Port, Destination Port/Via Port, Carrier, Effective Date, Expiry Date, and at least one of 20D Rate, 40D rate, 40HC Rate.
Rates — optional: Rate Type, Inland Delivery Location, Commodity type, Contract Number, Contract Owner, Notes 1.
Leave a price cell empty when that container size has no rate. Do not type 0.
Dates in a template are YYYY-MM-DD. Prices are USD. Validity is not imported.

Sailings — required: Carrier, Departure Port, Departure.
Sailings — optional: Arrival, Arrival Port, Transit Time, Vessel, Voyage, Service.
Arrival, when present, cannot be earlier than departure.

Apple Numbers files are not supported. Export the file to Excel (.xlsx). CSV and Excel (.xlsx) are the formats this screen accepts.
